About this course

AI & LLM Systems 101 is an internal training aid that explains the concepts and technology behind modern AI and Large Language Model (LLM) systems in plain English.

Who it's for

Everyone in IT — help desk, sysadmins, network and security staff, developers, and IT managers. It assumes you are technical but not a machine-learning specialist. The goal is to give you enough grounding to confidently understand, evaluate, and discuss AI tools with colleagues, vendors, and end users. It will not turn you into an ML researcher, and it doesn't try to.

How to use it

  • Work through the 12 modules in order. Each is designed to be skimmable in about 10–15 minutes.
  • Each module ends with a short quiz. Score 75% or higher to mark the module complete. You can retry as many times as you like — the explanations are part of the learning.
  • Your home page shows an overall progress bar and which modules you've completed. There's a "reset progress" button there too.
  • Use the Glossary whenever a term is unfamiliar, and try the interactive demos in Modules 4, 6, and 7.
  • Toggle light/dark mode with the button in the top navigation; your choice is remembered on this browser.

Privacy

This course runs entirely in your browser. It makes no network requests, sends no data anywhere, and uses your browser's local storage only to remember your progress and theme on this device. Nothing you type into the demos leaves your machine.

Accessibility

The course uses semantic HTML landmarks, a logical heading order, keyboard-operable quizzes and demos, visible focus outlines, and a light/dark theme aimed at sufficient color contrast. If you find an accessibility issue, please flag it to the maintainers.

Disclaimer

This material is for general education only. It is vendor-neutral and intentionally avoids recommending specific commercial products. AI capabilities and best practices change quickly; treat the content as a conceptual foundation, not formal policy, legal, security, or compliance advice. Always follow your organization's own policies and consult the appropriate teams before adopting or deploying AI tools.

For contributors

This site is built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and runs directly from disk over file:// — no build tools, no server, no external dependencies. See the README in the repository for the full contributor guide and the rules that keep it offline-friendly. The list of modules lives in one place, js/course.js.
